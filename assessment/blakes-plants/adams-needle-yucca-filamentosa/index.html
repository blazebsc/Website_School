Adams Needle Yucca Filamentosa

Learn more about Adams Needle Yucca Filamentosa. This plant is a great addition to any indoor or outdoor garden.

Care Tips

  • Thrives in full sun
  • Highly drought-tolerant; water infrequently
  • Needs sandy or gravelly well-draining soil
  • Remove dead leaves at the base for neatness
